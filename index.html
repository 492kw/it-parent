Free basic checklist

Start with the controls you already have.

This checklist is a sensible discussion tool, not a substitute for device documentation or specialist advice.

Home network

  1. Write down each internet service, router and Wi-Fi network in use.
  2. Rename known devices so a parent can recognise them.
  3. Consider a separate child device group or guest network where the router supports it.
  4. Use simple schedules and filtering only after discussing the household rule they support.
  5. Review the result together and keep recovery access available to a responsible adult.

Phones and tablets

  1. For Android, review Google Family Link with the child’s supervised account.
  2. For iPhone and iPad, review Apple Family Sharing and Screen Time.
  3. Set downtime, app limits and content settings appropriate to the family’s agreement.
  4. Keep emergency, health, school and accessibility needs in mind before applying limits.
  5. Review settings regularly as the child’s needs and responsibilities change.

Trust and boundaries

Control should not mean hidden surveillance.

Families set the rules. IT Parent’s role is to explain the options, apply agreed settings and leave parents or guardians with understandable control.

Consent and authorityChanges are discussed with and approved by the responsible parent or guardian. A child’s safety, dignity and household context matter.
Passwords and accessDo not send passwords by email. Any temporary access needed for an agreed setup should be controlled by the account holder and changed or removed afterwards.
Data retentionOnly the practical notes needed for an agreed setup should be kept. Avoid collecting private content, full browsing histories or unnecessary account data.
Monitoring limitsNetwork and device controls can support household rules, but they do not replace conversation, supervision, safeguarding or professional advice.
Useful official references

Tools that may form part of the setup.

The right choice depends on a family’s devices, accounts and rules. Capability should be checked against the current official documentation before purchase or configuration.

Home network controls

A capable home router can make devices easier to identify and can support separate networks, schedules and filtering where appropriate.

Google Family Link

Google’s family controls can support supervised Android and Google accounts, including app approvals and time settings.

Apple Screen Time

Apple’s Family Sharing and Screen Time can support downtime, app limits and content settings on Apple devices.
